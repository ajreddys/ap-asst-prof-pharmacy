Exam strategy

When is a guess worth it?

With four options, a blind guess earns nothing on average. Rule out even one option and every guess starts paying. We train elimination on every mock.

ABCD
Blind guess (1 in 4)±0.00 per guess
ABCD
One option ruled out (1 in 3)+0.33 per guess
ABCD
Two ruled out (1 in 2)+1.00 per guess

Assumes four options per question. Expected marks = 3 × P(correct) − 1 × P(wrong).

Selection process

From CBT to interview

  1. 1

    Screening test (CBT) This course

    150 MCQs in 180 minutes, conducted by APPSC on 3 December. Qualifying marks: 40% General, 35% OBC, 30% SC/ST/PBD.

  2. 2

    12:1 shortlist

    Ranked on CBT marks per vacancy and category. Ties go to the older candidate.

  3. 3

    Academic and research score

    The university's Screening-cum-Evaluation Committee validates your academic and research record.

  4. 4

    4:1 interview list

    Shortlisted on the committee's merit list. If several universities shortlist you, you declare your preference order within 5 days.

  5. 5

    Interview

    Conducted by each university for its shortlisted candidates.
